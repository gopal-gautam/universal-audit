# Code of Conduct

Universal Audit is intended to be a practical, respectful, and welcoming open source project.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent, but accept corrections when impact differs from intent.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or conduct.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of issues, pull requests, or community spaces.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns by opening a private security advisory or contacting a project maintainer. Maintainers will review reports and respond with appropriate action, which may include warnings, moderation, or removal from project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards of acceptable behavior and may take fair corrective action in response to unacceptable behavior.
